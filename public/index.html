How it works

  1. The bot plays both decks for the opening turns, identically for everyone.
  2. You take over one deck at a pivotal moment — same board, same hand, every player.
  3. One scored attempt per day. Reloading resumes your run; it does not reroll it.
  4. Points come from how you win or lose: speed, interaction, protection, and combos.
  5. Practice is unlimited, random, and never touches the leaderboard or your streak.

What practice is for

  1. Learn a deck you have never piloted — combos, triggers and all.
  2. Test a line before you commit to it in a scored daily.
  3. See the scoring in action: the tracker explains every point as it lands.

Upload your own deck

Paste a Moxfield or Archidekt URL, or paste the decklist itself. The deck has to pass the same checks the built-in decks pass: 100 cards, legal commander, colour identity, bracket rules — and every card fully scripted, so it plays completely for every player. Cards the engine can't play completely are reported and swapped for equivalents automatically.

Community rotation

Verified uploads join the bracket they belong to and can come up in the daily or practice at any time.
